Unplugging the Mind: Non-Computational Cognition

Phase 1: The Audit
60:00
Dr. Robert Epstein's Challenge

Can you explain human behavior without using a single computer metaphor?

For half a century, we've viewed human minds through the lens of dominant technology: from Ancient Greek wax tablets to steam engines, to modern CPUs, RAM, and algorithms.

"If we view humans as organic software or biological hardware, we will design AI that treats humans like faulty computers."
