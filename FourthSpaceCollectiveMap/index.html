What is the Fourth Space?

Every other Tuesday in HCAI 2101 (inside the EPIC building), we come together as a learning community and enter into a "Fourth Space" environment.

"A Fourth Space is a space specifically designed for collective sensemaking around technological change. We are not escaping from AI here; we are encouraging encounters with it; mediated by presence and shared bewilderment, critical thinking and questioning."

Each session gives you time to build community, work on your SAI project, and most importantly, think. Use this navigator to explore suitable corners on UNC Charlotte's campus to think, code, or stroll during our class sessions.
