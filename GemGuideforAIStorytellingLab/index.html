STARS Core Framework Blueprint

Your custom Gem is configured to structure stories using the **STARS Framework**. When deploying, it forces the AI model to stop right before the final **"S"**, ensuring your team does the critical translation.

S
Situation - Define near-future societal, technical context.
T
Task - Introduce ethical challenge, dilemma, or socio-technical friction.
A
Action - How did humans and AI collaborate or fight to solve it?
R
Result - Deep, messy fables showing unexpected outcomes.
S
Strategy (Student Driven) - Bridge to real-world AI design principle!
