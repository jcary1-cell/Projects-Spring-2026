What is a Campfire Session?

"Picture a real campfire: People pull up a log, gather in a circle, and share compelling stories. The atmosphere is collaborative, and the conversation flows naturally."

In our lab, we ditch the podium. Instead of talking at your peers, you are leading a dynamic, community-driven conversation with them.

The Vibe: Conversational & Collaborative

  • Informal: Think of it as a guided conversation.
  • The Circle: Everyone is on the same level.
  • The Tech: Keep devices tucked away unless actively used for your pitch.

Your Narrative: Ignite the Conversation

  • Own Your Narrative: Keep it 5-7 minutes. Focus on human context and ethics.
  • Be Conversational: Use natural body language and eye contact.
  • Welcome the Messiness: Don't strive for a perfect solution—highlight the dilemmas.

Active Engagement: Keep the Fire Burning

  • Listen with Intent: Put away distractions; focus on the human stakes.
  • Constructive Questions: Ask about ethical guardrails and community impact.
  • "Glows and Grows": Share one deep resonance (glow) and one area for deeper critical thinking (grow).
