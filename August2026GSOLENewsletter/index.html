Preparing for the Fall Horizon.

President’s Message

Dear GSOLE Members,

As August arrives, we enter that energetic time of transition when educators around the globe prepare for the upcoming academic year. August is a moment to synthesize new ideas, refresh online course design, and reconnect with our vibrant professional community before the rush of the fall term.

Following our leadership transitions last month, our incoming team is officially in full swing! We spent early August hosting our monthly Cyber Salon where President Kevin DePew engaged with members to hear your direct feedback, visions, and priorities for the future of online literacy instruction (OLI). Your insights are actively helping shape GSOLE’s strategic agenda for the next two years.

Planning for the 2027 GSOLE Annual Conference (February 3–5, 2027) is moving forward at full speed. As we prepare to launch our second decade as an organization, we invite all members to help shape this hallmark event. Whether you have capacity to assist during the proposal review phase, help organize plenary sessions, or serve as a virtual session moderator, your voice is essential.

In this August issue, we are also thrilled to highlight our **2026 Research Grant updates**, share fresh publications in *Research in Online Literacy Education (ROLE)* and *OLOR Effective Practices*, and invite submission proposals for our fall calls.

We wish you all a reflective, productive, and smooth transition into the Fall 2026 semester!

President

Kevin DePew

ROLE Call for Proposals: AI & OWI

Deadline November 01, 2026

Research in Online Literacy Education (ROLE) invites article proposal submissions for our upcoming peer-reviewed issue. This special issue engages deeply with the rapidly expanding conversations surrounding Artificial Intelligence (AI), generative writing tools, automated feedback systems, and critical digital literacy in online writing instruction (OWI).

Recommended Proposal Topics:

  • Ethical AI integration & policy development in virtual composition classrooms
  • Equitable assessment & student agency in AI-augmented writing environments
  • Empirical investigations of student and instructor experiences with LLMs

Regular Community Space

GSOLE Cyber Salon Sessions

Cyber Salons are informal, monthly virtual gatherings held on the first Tuesday of every month at 3:00 PM EST. They provide open spaces for online literacy educators to discuss emerging pedagogies, troubleshoot digital classroom challenges, and network. Free and open to both members and non-members!
